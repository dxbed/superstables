# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: January 8, 2025_

This Cookie Policy explains how Superstables uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and platform. By using our Service, you agree to the use of cookies as outlined in this policy.

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site recognize your device and store information about your preferences or past actions. We use cookies to enhance your experience on our off-exchange clearing and settlement platform.

## Types of Cookies We Use

- **Essential Cookies**  
  Necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core features such as security, network management, and accessibility. These cookies are required for our financial services platform to operate securely.

- **Analytics Cookies**  
  Help us understand how visitors interact with the site by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This helps us improve our services and user experience.

- **Functionality Cookies**  
  Allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your username, language preferences, or dashboard settings) and provide enhanced features for our platform.

- **Security Cookies**  
  Used to protect against fraud and ensure the security of our financial services platform. These cookies help us verify your identity and prevent unauthorized access.

- **Performance Cookies**  
  Collect information about how you use our website, such as which pages you visit most often, to help us optimize our platform's performance.

## How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for several purposes:

- **Authentication**: To keep you logged in to your account securely
- **Security**: To protect against fraud and unauthorized access
- **Preferences**: To remember your settings and preferences
- **Analytics**: To understand how our platform is used and improve our services
- **Compliance**: To ensure we meet regulatory requirements for financial services

## Third-Party Cookies

We may also use third-party cookies from trusted partners who provide services to us, such as:

- Analytics providers to help us understand website usage
- Security providers to protect against fraud
- Payment processors to facilitate transactions

## Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through settings. You can usually configure your browser to:

- Accept or reject all cookies
- Notify you when a cookie is set
- Delete cookies manually or automatically
- Block third-party cookies specifically

### Browser-Specific Instructions

- **Chrome**: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox**: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari**: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
- **Edge**: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

## Impact of Disabling Cookies

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our platform:

- Essential cookies are required for basic platform functionality
- Disabling functionality cookies may require you to re-enter preferences
- Some features may not work properly without cookies
